Field Work In validation

ShelfSense: shelf intelligence from a walk down the aisle.

An in-house build, currently being tested against real retail bays. One pass with a handheld phone and the bay reports itself — which lanes are stocked, which are empty, and which the scan could not read.

Built for shelves nobody has instrumented.

Out-of-stocks are found today by someone walking the aisle with a clipboard, or not found at all. ShelfSense keeps the walk and removes the clipboard.

  • No hardware in the store

    Nothing mounted to the fixture, nothing wired into store systems, no shelf sensors to maintain. The capture device is a phone that staff already carry.

  • No setup per bay

    No planogram upload and no per-store configuration. A bay the system has never seen still produces a structured report.

  • It says when it cannot tell

    Lanes the pass did not cover well enough are returned as unreadable rather than quietly guessed. An honest gap in the report is worth more than a confident wrong answer.

  • A deliverable, not a dashboard

    Each scan produces a per-lane report and a one-page summary an area manager can act on without logging into anything.

ShelfSense is being measured against hand counts taken at the shelf. We will publish accuracy figures here once they are numbers we would stand behind in front of a client — not before.
